Light on a hex sphere

Two things the sphere changes about lighting: six lateral neighbours instead of four, and a sun direction that makes day and night a place.

One layer only, so these are discs rather than balls. A hex disc holds 3r²+3r+1 cells against 2r²+2r+1 on squares — the ratio walks up to 1.5. At a pentagon a ring holds 5k instead of 6k, so the disc keeps 5/6 of its area. The light is not dimmer there; there is simply less world inside the same radius.
