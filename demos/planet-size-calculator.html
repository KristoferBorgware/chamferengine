Block size is measured flat-to-flat; a hexagon that wide covers ~0.87× the footprint of a square block of the same size, and is ~1.15× wider corner-to-corner. Each level up doubles the radius but quadruples the cell count. Voxel totals assume a full crust — with on-demand generation you only store what players change.
